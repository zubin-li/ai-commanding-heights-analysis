## 📌 Executive Summary & Core Economic Thesis

This project provides an exhaustive empirical audit and McKinsey/Goldman Sachs-grade strategic presentation deck based on the seminal 145-page industry report **"The Commanding Heights: Foundation Model Commoditization & The Real Reallocation of Economic Rent"** by Will Manidis & Joe Roberts.

We cross-verify the report's controversial theses and rampant social media distortions against authoritative, primary-source empirical data from **Epoch AI, Apollo Global Management, the U.S. Census Bureau, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), Stanford HAI, and SEC Form 10-K filings**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 💡 **Core Economic Iron Law: The 7% Inventor's Curse & Economic Rent Transfer**  
> Five hundred years of commercial history (from the Watt steam engine, Edison's electricity, the commercial PC, to Internet Web 1.0) rigorously demonstrate that **inventors of fundamental general-purpose technologies capture only 5% to 9% of the total economic surplus they unlock. Over 90% is redistributed downstream to consumers and captured by monopolistic bottlenecks controlling the true "Commanding Heights."**  
> 
> Foundation AI models are experiencing this commoditization crisis at hyper-speed. Driven by algorithmic convergence and open-weight competition, raw token prices are plunging by 13x per year (47% quarterly). As models become ubiquitous, low-margin utilities, economic rent is inexorably shifting toward:
> 1. **Physical Energy & Utility Grid Interconnects** (Megawatt-scale access);
> 2. **Proprietary, Non-Public Kinetic/Physical Datasets** (closed-loop operational history);
> 3. **Incumbent Enterprise Systems of Record (SoR)** (high switching costs & compliance);
> 4. **Accountable Human-AI Symbiotes** who hold legal and fiduciary liability in the real world.
